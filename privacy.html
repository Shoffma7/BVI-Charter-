BVI Crew: Privacy Policy

BVI Crew is a private planning app for one group trip (a British Virgin Islands boat charter, February 2027). It is not offered to the public.

What the app stores

Name, cabin, phone number, expected arrival and landing times, gear claims, poll votes, and trip-fund entries that members type in. If you allow notifications, it also stores a device notification token linked to your name.

Where it is stored

In a Google Firebase database used only by this app. Other members of the group can see the crew list and trip entries. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Notifications

Used only for trip updates such as polls, plan changes and expense approvals. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

Deleting your data

Ask the trip organizer, Spencer Hoffman, to remove your entry. Data is deleted after the trip.
